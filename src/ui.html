Get your page ready

Screen Ready turns the frames on this page into one PDF a screen reader can follow, one frame per page.

  • Only what you're exporting. Move drafts, notes and old versions to another page, or hide them. Every visible frame is a page.
  • In reading order. Pages go left to right, then top to bottom. You can reorder them later.
  • Or select a few. Select just the frames you want before you start.

Then: Review what needs a look, hear it in Preview, and Export.

For the best reading

  • Auto layout for columns and stacks. They're read in flow order.
  • Keep things together. Put a chart's title and source inside its frame, a caption with its photo.
  • Text styles for headings. Set a role once per style under Review → Options.
  • Live text, not outlines. Outlined words need alt text to be heard.
  • Name images for what they show. “Map of India” becomes the draft alt text.
  • Hide what shouldn't be read. Hidden layers and frames are left out.
